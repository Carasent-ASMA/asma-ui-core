ASMA-8600: `StyledAccordionDetails` gains an opt-in `toolbarHeader` prop for an accordion whose header is a `DynamicToolbar` directly above a table. It sets `data-toolbar-header` on the content element, and the content's padding-top then follows the header so the controls-to-table gap is the Figma 16 px: `0` for an `inline` toolbar with the large 72 px summary (the row already leaves 16 px under the 40 px controls), `12px` for the small 48 px summary (4 px + 12), `1rem` for every other layout (`compact`, `stacked`, `selection-split`, where the controls are the last row), and `1rem` for an `inline` toolbar with helper text (the helper row sits under the controls, so the table is 16 px under the helper text). `StyledAccordionSummary` now exposes `data-size` on its button and `DynamicToolbar` exposes `data-has-helper` on its root, for the content rule to read. The default is unchanged: without the prop the content keeps `padding: 0.5rem 1rem 1rem 1rem`. The rules are literal declarations in `StyledAccordion.module.scss` (no `!important`), and a consumer `contentClassName` with an important padding utility still wins.
